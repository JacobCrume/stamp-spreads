# STAMP identity

The mark is the word STAMP in capitals. There is no symbol.

The letters are a custom drawing based on the Toundra wordmark: cap height 338, stem 110, narrow counters, round feet, semicircular arches. Manrope is the text face for pack lines, the site, and the plan. It is not the wordmark.

## Colour

One pair for every flavour. Both colours are purple, in the same relationship as Toundra's dark green on lime: a bright field, a dark ink. Flavour is set in Manrope. It does not get its own colour.

| Role | Hex | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light purple | `#E24BFF` | The bright field, and the wordmark when it sits on the dark field. The lime's role. |
| Dark purple | `#3B0764` | The ink, and the dark field. The forest green's role. |
