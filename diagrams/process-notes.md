# Sheet making and release

Figure: `diagrams/process.png`, from `diagrams/process.mmd`.

The pass path is order in, batch deposit onto film, cut, nest, check, pack, dispatch. At the check, a stamp outside 10 g ± 0.5 g, or a film that will not release, goes to Reject. That path stops. It does not join Pack or Dispatch, and the stamp does not ship.

## Bottlenecks

**1. Deposit weight.** Peanut paste and yeast extract thicken or slacken while they sit in the hopper, so the depositor drifts outside 10 g ± 0.5 g and the check rejects a run of discs.

Response: stop the depositor. Weigh the next five discs. Restart only when all five sit between 9.5 g and 10.5 g. Discs that failed are rejected and are not nested.

**2. Release film.** A roll that will not peel fails only after spread is already on it, so one bad roll can reject a whole deposit run before pack.

Response: peel-test the first eight discs of each new roll. If one will not release, stop, quarantine that roll, and reject every stamp already on it. Load a fresh roll and test eight more before nesting continues. Nothing from the failed roll is packed or dispatched.
