## Why the process fits a film-and-sheet format

Griffiths & Co contract-manufacture and pack liquid and dry foods. The services page names sauces, salad dressings and dips, and it lists two small film formats:

- Sachets, liquid and dry, 5 g to 1.5 kg, on printed film or plain film.
- Pottles / portion-control units, liquid and dry, 10 g to 200 g.

It also says they keep a range of films to lengthen shelf life, and that they fill sauces, dressings and dips.

A Stamp is a 10 g disc of spread on release film, eight discs nested on a card. The matching step is the small film portion: a set weight of a paste put onto film at about 10 g, then packed. Dips, sauces and dressings are the same class of paste as a toast spread, and 10 g sits inside both ranges they print.

The services page does not say they already nest eight peel-off discs on a printed card. The card is a pack built on that film portion, not a format the page claims they run today.
