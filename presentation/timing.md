## Part 2: Personal Reflection Speaking Cues (1:04 – 2:45)

*Deliver in your personal voice. AI assistance was strictly prohibited for reflection text.*

### Slide 11: Negative Constraints (1:04 – 1:18)
- In steering AI models, I enforced strict negative prompt constraints against fake certification badges and therapeutic health claims to prevent hallucinations.

### Slide 12: Session Partitioning (1:18 – 1:30)
- I partitioned distinct modular sessions for data cleaning, branding, and copywriting, which eliminated context drift across the project.

### Slide 13: Turning Point (1:30 – 1:45)
- My pivotal moment was catching an unrealistic AI assumption that churned dairy butter could sit ambiently without spoilage. I intervened to formulate a shelf-stable cultured dairy spread.

### Slide 14: Verification (1:45 – 1:58)
- I treated the AI as a drafter, not a director. I audited raw scan data outliers and manually verified shelf prices on New World instead of trusting model summaries.

### Slide 15: Authenticity (1:58 – 2:15)
- To combat AI slop, I stripped corporate buzzwords and marketing hype in favour of plain, punchy facts, and grounded packaging in real continuous deposit tooling.

### Slide 16: Food Regulation (2:15 – 2:30)
- In food and beverage, AI hallucinations are a severe legal liability. I enforced strict FSANZ compliance and ensured life-threatening allergen warnings were never obscured.

### Slide 17: Human Accountability (2:30 – 2:45)
- Ultimately, accountability can never be outsourced to an algorithm. Generative AI accelerates drafting velocity, but human skepticism, taste, and moral ownership remain essential.
