## Recording Checklist & Practical Tips

1. **PowerPoint Teleprompter Mode:**
   - In PowerPoint, click **Slide Show** → **Record Slide Show**.
   - Select **Teleprompter View** at the top of the screen.
   - The slide presenter notes will scroll right below your webcam, allowing you to maintain direct eye contact while speaking naturally.
2. **Audio & Video Setup:**
   - Position your camera at eye level with good frontal lighting (facing a window or desk lamp).
   - Use an external microphone or headset if available to ensure crisp, clean audio.
3. **Pacing & Timing:**
   - Aim for a brisk, enthusiastic tone during Part 1 (pitch), then shift to a thoughtful, reflective tone for Part 2 (reflection).
   - Expected running time: **~2 minutes 45 seconds**. This is well within the 2–3 minute target and gives a safe 44-second buffer before the 3m29s hard cutoff.
4. **Export & Verification:**
   - Go to `File` → `Export As...` → choose `MP4` format and `1080p Full HD`.
   - Verify that the exported file plays smoothly with webcam overlay and slides visible.
   - Upload the `.mp4` file directly to Learn (no streaming links).
