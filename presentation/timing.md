## Part 1: Investor Pitch Script (0:00 – 1:35)

### Slide 01: Cover (0:00 – 0:07)
> "Kia ora. I'm Jacob, and this is Stamp: a New Zealand breakfast spread innovation engineered for hot toast."

### Slide 02: Problem — Jars Require Infrastructure (0:07 – 0:14)
> "Eating toast away from a full residential kitchen is fundamentally broken."

### Slide 03: Problem — Mobile Friction (0:14 – 0:21)
> "In an office, student hall, or flat, nobody wants to lug a sticky 500-gram jar, hunt for a knife, and wash it in a shared sink."

### Slide 04: Problem — The Unmet Need (0:21 – 0:28)
> "Because jars are tethered to kitchens, consumers compromise on dry cereal bars or skip breakfast altogether."

### Slide 05: Solution — All 4 Flavours Showcase (0:28 – 0:36)
> "Stamp reinvents the category: calibrated 10-gram spread discs mounted on release film across four iconic kiwi favourites—Salted Butter, Raspberry, Savoury Yeast, and Peanut."

### Slide 06: Gesture — Zero Cutlery / Thermal Melt (0:36 – 0:43)
> "You peel off the release liner and press it directly onto hot bread. Residual thermal energy melts it into an even spread in seconds—zero utensils or mess."

### Slide 07: Format — 12-Month Ambient Stability (0:43 – 0:50)
> "Engineered with high-stability emulsions for 12 months of ambient pantry and bag storage in pocket-sized 8-stamp card sheets."

### Slide 08: Market Scale — $341M Category (0:50 – 0:57)
> "Cleaned retail scan data shows the New Zealand spreads category expanded 24% to 341 million dollars."

### Slide 09: Market Trend — Premium Surging +29% (0:57 – 1:05)
> "Within the category, premium branded spreads surged 29% to 113 million dollars, massively outperforming commodity tiers as consumers trade up."

### Slide 10: Competitive Void — 0 Rivals (1:05 – 1:12)
> "Across 154 supermarket spreads, every single product is a bulk jar. Not one offers a utensil-free portion format."

### Slide 11: Unit Economics — 35% Gross Margin (1:12 – 1:19)
> "At five dollars for an 8-pack—just 63 cents a serving—we retain a strong $1.75 gross margin on contract packaging to fund expansion."

### Slide 12: Manufacturing — Contract Tooling (1:19 – 1:27)
> "Production leverages proven contract rotary-die deposition lines onto food-grade release liner, eliminating custom machinery outlay."

### Slide 13: Operations — Lean Capex Pilot (1:27 – 1:35)
> "By partnering with established contract packers in Auckland, we validate consumer demand with minimal initial capex."

## Part 2: Personal Reflection Speaking Cues (1:35 – 2:45)

*Deliver in your personal voice. AI assistance was strictly prohibited for reflection text.*

### Slide 14: Negative Constraints (1:35 – 1:48)
- In steering AI models, I enforced strict negative prompt constraints against fake certification badges and miracle health claims to prevent regulatory hallucinations.

### Slide 15: Modular Architecture (1:48 – 2:00)
- I partitioned distinct modular sessions for data cleaning, branding, and copywriting, which eliminated context drift and hallucinated metrics across the project.

### Slide 16: The Turning Point: Spoilage (2:00 – 2:12)
- My pivotal turning point was catching an unrealistic AI assumption that churned dairy butter could sit ambiently without spoilage. Human intervention forced reformulation to a shelf-stable dairy emulsion.

### Slide 17: AI as Drafter, Not Director (2:12 – 2:22)
- I treated AI as a drafter, not a director. I audited raw supermarket scan outliers and manually verified shelf prices on New World instead of trusting model summaries.

### Slide 18: Combating AI Slop (2:22 – 2:30)
- To combat AI slop, I stripped corporate buzzwords and marketing hype in favour of plain, punchy facts: Peel. Press. Toast.

### Slide 19: Regulatory Safety & FSANZ (2:30 – 2:38)
- In food and beverage, AI hallucinations are a severe legal and health liability. I enforced strict FSANZ compliance and ensured mandatory allergen warnings were prominent.

### Slide 20: Human Accountability (2:38 – 2:45)
- Ultimately, accountability can never be outsourced to an algorithm. Generative AI accelerates drafting velocity, but human discernment, taste, and moral ownership remain essential.
